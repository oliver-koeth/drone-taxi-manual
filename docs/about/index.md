# About Aetherwings

## Project Brief

**Aetherwings GmbH** is a Munich-based urban air mobility operator founded in 2025 with the vision of making city-level aerial transport a safe, sustainable, and everyday reality. Our fleet of autonomous electric vertical takeoff and landing (eVTOL) aircraft serves metropolitan corridors across Central Europe, offering a faster, cleaner alternative to ground-based taxis and rideshares.

We operate under **EASA SC-VTOL** certification and are the first European drone-taxi operator to receive a blanket operational license for premium urban passenger routes in Germany, Austria, and Switzerland.

### Our Mission

> *To make the sky the fastest lane of the city — zero emissions, maximum safety, always autonomous.*

### Safety Record

Aetherwings has maintained a **zero-incident operational record** across all 1,200+ logged flight hours since commencing passenger trials in late 2025. Every flight is monitored in real time by our ground-based Supervision Center (GSC) in Munich, with redundant command-and-control links and geofenced operational volumes.
